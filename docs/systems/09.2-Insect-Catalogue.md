# Purpose

This document catalogues insect and invertebrate species in Project Draugr.

Insects are not wildlife and not flora.

They are their own ecological layer — decomposers, pollinators, disease vectors, food sources, and material producers.

A world without insects is a world without pollination, without decomposition, and without the base of the food web.

---

# Schema Reference

Insects are implemented through two tables introduced in V40:

- `insect_colony` — extends world_object; represents a physical colony with health, product readiness, and disturbance state
- `ecology_site.site_category = 'INSECT'`

Insect colonies are physical world objects. They can be disturbed, damaged, and destroyed. They recover over time.

# Colony Types

## Honeybee Hive

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| colony_kind | `honeybee_hive` |
| biome_affinity | FOREST, HIGHLAND, SCRUB |
| activity_cycle | DIURNAL |
| season_active | SPRING_SUMMER_AUTUMN |

**Products:**

| item_key | condition |
|----------|-----------|
| raw_honey | RAID_HIVE, product_ready_at elapsed |
| beeswax | RAID_HIVE alongside honey |
| propolis | RAID_HIVE, rare yield |

**Mechanics:**
- RAID_HIVE without smoke → sting injury (severity 8–15, multiple stings possible)
- RAID_HIVE with smoke (active fire nearby, smoke described in action text) → sting injury suppressed
- Colony health reduces on raid → regrowth timer before next product batch
- Presence in chunk → flora regrowth rate +30% (pollination)
- Colony health = 0 → colony destroyed, site inactive until natural respawn

## Mosquito Swarm

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| colony_kind | `mosquito_swarm` |
| biome_affinity | WETLAND, RIVERBANK, LAKE_EDGE |
| activity_cycle | CREPUSCULAR (dawn and dusk peaks) |
| season_active | SPRING_SUMMER_AUTUMN |

**Products:** None harvestable.

**Mechanics:**
- Passive hazard: chronicle spending time near wetland at dawn or dusk without repellent → illness_severity +3 per hour of exposure
- Repellents: smoke (active fire within 2 chunks), wild_garlic (worn/applied), certain herb applications
- Dragonfly colony in same chunk → mosquito swarm effect reduced 50%
