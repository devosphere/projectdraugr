# 11.1 – Species Registry

> **Project Draugr**
>
> *A world full of life, organized by the life it contains.*

---

# Purpose

This document is the canonical species registry for all wildlife and monster species in Project Draugr.

Each entry records: `species_key`, ecological role, movement class, activity cycle, biome affinity, size tier, resistance value, tamability score (if applicable), drops, and FSM behavioral notes.

Monsters are listed separately at the end, distinguished by their `ecology_site.site_category = 'MONSTER'`.

---

# Size Tiers

| Tier | Example range | Notes |
|------|--------------|-------|
| XS | rabbit, squirrel, frog | Cannot injure a chronicle seriously |
| S | fox, badger, hare | Minor injury at most |
| M | deer, boar, wildcat | Moderate injury potential |
| L | wolf, bear, elk | Serious injury potential |
| XL | cave_bear, monster tier | Can kill in one to two encounters |

---

# Movement Classes

| Class | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| TERRESTRIAL | Ground-bound |
| AERIAL | Primarily airborne; melee confrontation only if grounded |
| AQUATIC | Water-bound; FISH intent applies |
| AMPHIBIOUS | Both land and water |
