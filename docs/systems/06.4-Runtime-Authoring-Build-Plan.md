## The decision, in one paragraph (do not re-litigate — see DR-0021)

A bounded, multi-agent **runtime action-authoring pipeline** lets a chronicle's realistic real-world
procedures resolve against the world's mechanics — including inventing mechanics the world lacks — with
**no stochastic step ever writing an outcome or breaking shared canon.** Governing invariant: **every AI
role is optional and non-load-bearing over a complete deterministic core.** Five roles: Narrator (witness,
never hints), Interpreter (on miss, compose *existing* processes only), Architect (author **per-chronicle
scoped data rows** — never schema/canon/outcomes), QA Critic (independent model; plausibility only; ≤2-round
loop), Auditor (deterministic hot-path integrity + occasional LLM anomaly pass; read-only). Two-tier
sandbox→canon; canon promotion is human-gated via Overseer Tech Discovery Queue → GitHub. Cost converges to
zero per mechanic after the patch; the ~99% common case never calls an AI.
