# Purpose

This document records major architectural, gameplay, technical, and creative decisions made throughout the development of Project Draugr.

Its purpose is to preserve the reasoning behind important decisions so future contributors understand **why** a decision was made—not merely **what** was decided.

This document serves as the project's institutional memory.

## DR-0001

**Date**

2026-07

**Category**

Vision

**Status**

Accepted

### Decision

Project Draugr is fundamentally a **persistent world simulation**, not an AI-first project.

### Reasoning

Early design iterations centered heavily around large-scale artificial intelligence, where nearly every entity would eventually possess its own intelligence.

Although aligned with the long-term vision, this approach made the MVP financially and technically unrealistic.

The project was re-centered around a persistent world simulation.

Artificial intelligence became a future enhancement rather than the project's foundation.

### Impact

- Simplified MVP scope.
- Reduced technical complexity.
- Preserved long-term vision.
- Established "Simulation First" philosophy.

## DR-0008

**Date**

2026-07-29

**Category**

Persistence

**Status**

Accepted

### Decision

The implementation uses PostgreSQL runtime state plus a separate immutable event archive. Physical objects use UUID identities and transition lifecycle state rather than being deleted.

### Reasoning

The world requires authoritative current state without sacrificing permanent historical continuity. Runtime queries and historical recording have different operational needs, so they must be stored and governed separately.

### Impact

- A database migration owns every schema change.
- `world_object` retains identity through moves, consumption, and destruction.
- Every active object is constrained to one location or owner.
- `world_event` rejects updates and deletes at the database boundary.
- Future adaptive civilization domains are additive migrations, introduced only when the simulation establishes a need.

## DR-0012

**Date**

2026-07-31

**Category**

Persistence

**Status**

Accepted

### Decision

The world is ONE shared persistent entity. When a chronicle dies, all `world_object` rows they created or gathered remain in the world unless they naturally decay. The new chronicle spawns at a random location in the same world. Objects, constructions, and literature from previous chronicles are discoverable legacy.

### Reasoning

Permanent death would be meaningless if the world reset with each life. The world's history — built structures, written documents, scattered tools — must persist as the accumulated legacy of all chronicles. A new chronicle finding a dead predecessor's stone archive is not a reward mechanic; it is the natural consequence of a world that never forgets.

### Impact

- `world_object` rows are never deleted for any reason. Lifecycle state transitions to DESTROYED; the row remains.
- New chronicles spawn at a random location, making retrieval of a prior chronicle's legacy a matter of exploration and luck, not a guaranteed restart bonus.
- Literature becomes the most powerful legacy mechanic: knowledge written on stone slabs or bark sheets survives and is readable by any future chronicle who finds it.
- Constructions decay per the existing integrity system but are never hard-deleted.

## DR-0013

**Date**

2026-07-31

**Category**

Technical Architecture

**Status**

Accepted

### Decision

Chronicle capability (what a chronicle knows) is per-chronicle and dies with the chronicle. DB schema (which domains exist as tables) is monotonic and global — new domains are added once, for all time, the first time that domain is invented anywhere in the world's history.

### Reasoning

Two kinds of "knowledge" exist in the system and must not be confused. `chronicle_capability_adaptation` rows represent a body's muscle memory and practiced skill — they belong to one life and end with it. Database tables for a domain (e.g. pottery kilns, smithing forges) must exist globally the moment any object of that domain exists in the world, because a future chronicle may find those objects and interact with them. Branching schemas per chronicle would make it impossible for a new chronicle to use a dead chronicle's forge even if they stand in front of it.

### Impact

- When a chronicle dies, their familiarity scores are gone. A new chronicle starts at zero even in a world full of functional infrastructure.
- When a new domain (pottery, smithing, agriculture) is invented for the first time anywhere, the Persistent State Architect adds the required tables ONCE via a Flyway migration. All future chronicles inherit those tables in the schema.
- The distinction: you can FIND a dead chronicle's kiln (world_object row persists). You cannot INHERIT their skill (chronicle_capability_adaptation does not transfer).

## DR-0014

**Date**

2026-07-31

**Category**

Artificial Intelligence

**Status**

Accepted

### Decision

Before any AI agent is introduced, six foundation items must exist: (F1) a structured perception/context frame as the AI seam, (F2) tick progression deltas surfaced in that frame, (F3) ATTENTION-scaled world detail, (F4) all death vectors fully wired, (F5) a complete Auditor invariant catalog, (F6) Auditor runtime scheduling and endpoint. Only after F1–F6 are complete will the Simulation Agent (AI narration) be wired as Task #21.

### Reasoning

An AI agent operating on incomplete or inconsistent context will narrate incorrect states, hallucinate missing physiology, and undermine the witness-stance contract. The context frame (F1) is the seam — every AI reads from it. If the seam does not exist yet, adding an AI agent produces noise, not narration. The Auditor (F5/F6) must be complete before any AI can mutate state, because the Auditor is the only safety net.

### Impact

- Task #21 (AI narration) is explicitly blocked until F1–F4 are done.
- The Persistent State Architect is blocked until F7 (domain-creation pattern) and the F8 schema decision are complete. F8 is now decided (DR-0013).
- Build order: F1 → F4 → F2/F3 → F5/F6 → F7 → wire AIs.

## DR-0015

**Date**

2026-07-31

**Category**

Artificial Intelligence

**Status**

Accepted

### Decision

Before wiring AI narration (Task #21), the deterministic ecosystem must reach maximum coverage: full species registries across all kingdoms (flora, insects, land wildlife, aquatic, aerial, monsters), behavioral FSM with cascade rules, and a NarrationEngine producing quality witness-stance prose for every intent×outcome combination. AI narration is introduced only after this foundation is complete.

### Reasoning

AI API calls are metered per token. Every action that can be narrated deterministically eliminates a token cost forever. The ecosystem expansion (V39–V46) covers all standard player interactions — gathering flora, encountering wildlife, fishing, tracking, taming — at zero marginal cost. A NarrationEngine ensures the deterministic path produces quality prose. When AI narration is finally wired, 90%+ of player actions already route to the deterministic path, making the AI financially sustainable at any player volume.

### Impact

- Migrations V39–V46 (flora, insects, expanded land wildlife, aerial + aquatic species, monsters, passive encounters, taming, lure/traps) precede Task #21.
- NarrationEngine precedes NarrationRouter.
- AI narration scope is bounded to: death (any cause), monster encounters (MONSTER-category species), WRITE/EDIT_DOCUMENT, OBSERVE with attention=HIGH, bond reaching TAMED stage, first discovery of another chronicle's objects, and action text > 300 chars with a non-trivial intent.

## DR-0016

**Date**

2026-07-31

**Category**

Artificial Intelligence / Narration

**Status**

Accepted

### Decision

The NarrationEngine generates complete, factually correct, witness-stance prose for every action. When the AI narration layer is invoked, it receives the existing backend prose plus the PerceptionFrame and contributes one to two sentences of atmospheric or sensory flavor. It does not generate narration from scratch.

### Reasoning

Full AI generation from a raw PerceptionFrame costs approximately 800 output tokens per call. Refinement of existing prose costs approximately 60 output tokens — roughly 13× cheaper per meaningful moment. The backend prose covers all facts (species, wound severity tier, behavioral outcome, location). The AI adds only what deterministic code cannot produce: texture, dread, atmosphere, sensory immediacy. This architecture also means the game ships with complete, correct narration on day one. AI is the upgrade layer, not the dependency.

### Impact

- NarrationEngine is a first-class production system, not a placeholder fallback.
- The PerceptionFrame gains a `backendNarration` field which the AI refinement call receives alongside the structured frame.
- AI output is bounded to one or two sentences maximum.
- The NarrationRouter (DR-0017) governs which actions receive AI refinement.
- If the Claude API is unavailable, the game continues functioning with backend narration unchanged.

## DR-0017

**Date**

2026-07-31

**Category**

Artificial Intelligence / Performance

**Status**

Accepted

### Decision

A `NarrationRouter` classifies every resolved action as `DETERMINISTIC` or `AI_REFINE` before any API call is attempted. The router reads signals already present in the PerceptionFrame: intent, attention level, outcome, sinceLastFrame delta count, species category (MONSTER vs WILDLIFE vs none), and action text length. DETERMINISTIC actions return the NarrationEngine prose unchanged. AI_REFINE actions proceed to the Claude API refinement call.

### Reasoning

Without a router, every player action incurs an API cost regardless of narrative significance. A player gathering clay twenty times in succession produces the same backend prose each time — no AI refinement is warranted. The router is the financial control layer that preserves sustainability at scale.

### Impact

- **Always DETERMINISTIC:** EQUIP, UNEQUIP, DROP, ADD_FUEL, DESIGNATE, MARK, GATHER_PLANT, FELL_TREE, FISH (terse), COLLECT_INSECTS, LURE, SET_TRAP, TRACK (no sign), any intent with attention=LOW and trivial outcome.
- **Always AI_REFINE:** death (any cause), any MONSTER-category species encounter, WRITE / EDIT_DOCUMENT, OBSERVE with attention=HIGH, bond reaching TAMED stage, first discovery of another chronicle's objects.
- **Conditional AI_REFINE:** action text > 300 characters with non-trivial intent; sinceLastFrame carries two or more state changes; CONFRONT with wound severity ≥ 35.
- The router is pure Java with no DB dependency — testable without Docker.

## DR-0018

**Date**

2026-07-31

**Category**

Simulation

**Status**

Accepted

### Decision

Wildlife and monster behavioral intelligence is implemented as an expanded finite state machine (FSM) with cascade rules between species. No language model is used for behavioral decisions. The FSM must produce emergent-feeling behavior — pack coordination, prey awareness cascades, scavenger attraction, seasonal aggression, ambush exemptions — through deterministic tick-based state transitions.

### Reasoning

AI-driven behavior is expensive, non-deterministic, and impossible to audit for correctness. A well-designed FSM with inter-species cascade rules produces behavior that appears intelligent without any token cost. Pack wolves coordinating, deer becoming alert when wolves hunt two chunks away, ravens converging on a fresh carcass — these all emerge from state machine rules evaluated on the ecology tick. Players cannot distinguish this from AI-driven behavior, and the simulation remains auditable and reproducible.

### Impact

- `wildlife_population.behavior_state` expands from 6 values to include STALKING, FEEDING, DRINKING, MIGRATING, NESTING, TERRITORIAL, PACK_HUNT.
- `WildlifeSimulationService` gains inter-species cascade evaluation: predator state changes trigger prey awareness shifts in adjacent chunks.
- Ambush hunter flag on wildcat and adder suppresses the ALERT signal before a passive attack.
- Seasonal modifiers applied per tick: winter → bear SHELTERING, autumn ungulates → TERRITORIAL.
- Scavenger cascade: any carcass created → raven/vulture/jackal populations shift to FORAGING toward that chunk.

## DR-0019

**Date**

2026-07-31

**Category**

Simulation / Narration

**Status**

Accepted

### Decision

All action text passes through an `ActionInputClassifier` before intent classification. Inputs classified as OBSCENE, NONSENSICAL, or PHYSICALLY_IMPOSSIBLE are intercepted and returned with deterministic narrator prose. They never reach the intent dispatcher, never consume AI tokens, and never produce a `chronicle_action` row.

### Reasoning

Players will submit sexual actions, profanity-directed actions, complete gibberish, and physically impossible commands. This is not an edge case — it is a certainty. Sending these to an AI narrator wastes tokens and produces responses that may be inappropriate or confusing. The simulation is a physics engine. The world responds to physical reality, not to sentiment or biology. A deterministic "the world continues without comment" response is both cheaper and more thematically consistent than any AI-generated reply.

### Impact

- `ActionInputClassifier.java` runs as the first step in `ChronicleActionService.resolve()`, before `classifyIntent()`.
- Classification categories: `VALID`, `PERSONAL_PHYSICAL_ACT`, `AGGRESSION_TOWARD_WILDLIFE`, `AGGRESSION_TOWARD_INANIMATE`, `NONSENSICAL`, `PHYSICALLY_IMPOSSIBLE`.
- `PERSONAL_PHYSICAL_ACT` (masturbation, sexual self-acts): **time passes, physiology changes, chronicle_action IS written**. Energy drains, hygiene degrades, calories burn. Repeated actions accumulate toward exhaustion and death — the only natural deterrent. Narrator witnesses flatly.
- `AGGRESSION_TOWARD_ENVIRONMENT` (sexual acts directed at wildlife, "fuck that [creature]"): triggers passive encounter logic — the creature responds to the physical provocation. Chronicle may be injured. Population shifts to ALERT.
- `AGGRESSION_TOWARD_INANIMATE` ("fuck that tree"): minor energy cost, flat narration, time passes (1 minute). No world state change beyond physiology tick.
- `NONSENSICAL` / `PHYSICALLY_IMPOSSIBLE`: no chronicle_action write, no tick, no AI call. The body has no shape for that intent.
- Narrator tone across all: flat, realistic, indifferent. No judgment, no humor, no moral stance. The world applies physics. That is all.

## DR-0020

**Date**

2026-08-01

**Category**

Simulation / Routing / Cost Control

**Status**

Accepted — implementation planned (see architecture/action-routing-hardening.md)

### Decision

Action resolution becomes two-phase. An `ActivityClassifier` first determines the **activity category** of an action (ACQUIRE, HUNT, PROCESS, CRAFT, CONSTRUCT, MAINTAIN, INHABIT, RECORD, OBSERVE, MOVE). Every downstream matcher — intent classification, the material-process resolver, and the ArchitectRouter — then matches **only within the vocabulary of that category**. Global, cross-module lexical matching is removed. Categories are carried as data on every matchable row and enforced by an Auditor invariant.

### Reasoning

The same defect recurred four times: unrelated modules matching on incidental words shared across domains. "gather mushrooms from the **forest**" matched `ore`; "**flashing**" matched `ash`; "**weatherproof**" matched the weather simulation domain; "**beams**" matched a timber keyword. Whole-word matching (d0e238c) removed those four specific cases but not the class, because the ambiguity is genuine — "dress the stone" and "dress the hide", "fire the pot" and "light a fire", "draw a map" and "draw water" are all legitimate whole words in two modules at once. Only knowing the activity category first disambiguates them.

The failure direction is what makes this urgent rather than cosmetic. A false negative is visible — the action does nothing and the player retries. A false positive is invisible and worse: a wrong COVERED suppresses the Architect call the situation needed, so the gap is never recorded, no schema is added, and the action becomes permanently unresolvable. Every such silent dead end is also a cost: the whole point of routing is to keep common actions off the AI, and a router that misroutes both fails to save the call and corrupts the foundation the Auditor then spends calls validating.

### Impact

- New `ActivityClassifier` runs before intent classification; deterministic, cached, no API cost.
- `activity_category` and `category_term` tables; every existing matchable row gains category columns, backfilled, with an Auditor invariant requiring at least one category per row (landing in the same migration as the columns).
- The 15 m² cabin simulation is adopted as a standing coverage fixture. Its current result — 0 of 12 steps covered, all INVENT — is the baseline the work must improve, and a regression fixture prevents coverage from silently falling.
- Exposed a deeper gap the routing fix does not close: `material_process` produces carried items, but a cabin is a sited, staged construction, and `construction_project` has only `progress_percent` with no notion of stage dependency. Staged construction (M3) is scoped separately.
- AI-assisted classification is explicitly out of scope. A router that spends a call to decide whether to spend a call defeats itself.

## DR-0021

**Date**

2026-08-03

**Category**

Artificial Intelligence / Simulation / Cost Control

**Status**

Accepted — implementation staged (spec to follow in architecture/runtime-procedure-authoring.md)

### Decision

Introduce a bounded, multi-agent **runtime action-authoring pipeline** so that a chronicle's realistic, real-world procedures can resolve against the world's mechanics — including inventing mechanics the world does not yet have — without a stochastic step ever writing an outcome or breaking the shared canon. This deliberately amends the prior rule ("AI belongs at authoring time, never at resolution time", routing-and-coverage-strategy.md) for the composition/authoring case, and leaves DR-0009 and DR-0013 intact: the runtime Architect writes **data rows within the existing schema**, never schema itself.

**The governing invariant (supreme over everything below):** *every AI role is optional and non-load-bearing over a complete deterministic core.* The game must run at full correctness on the deterministic layer with all AI disabled. No AI is ever the final authority on physics or canon.

**Five roles, each with a fixed boundary:**

1. **Simulation Agent (Narrator)** — pure witness. Narrates the attempt and the visible outcome, success or failure. **Never hints, teaches the procedure, or advises** (reaffirms DR-0010). The player brings the real-world knowledge; the game never hands it back.
2. **Procedure Interpreter** — read-only. Fires *only* on a deterministic classifier miss. Maps the player's procedure onto a **sequence of processes that already exist** (canonical + this chronicle's own). Every step is still validated by the deterministic gates (inputs, tools, mass balance, capability), which are the backstop against nearest-neighbour false-matches. Composes; never invents; never decides outcomes.
3. **Persistent State Architect** — authors a **new mechanic** only when the Interpreter cannot compose one. It writes **data rows scoped to the discovering chronicle** (`discovered_by_chronicle_id`), never schema, never canon. A genuinely new *domain* requiring new tables is out of runtime scope and remains a human-reviewed migration (DR-0013).
4. **QA Critic (Expert)** — independent reviewer, a model distinct from the Architect. Reviews the Architect's draft for **plausibility and balance** (design rule #5 — the judgment axis the deterministic gate cannot check), **never physics** (mass balance/reachability stay in code). Runs a bounded **author↔critic loop of at most 2 rounds**; on non-convergence the mechanic is not created this session and the attempt drops to the human-authoring backlog.
5. **Persistent State Auditor** — the integrity backstop, split by capability: a **deterministic per-action gate on the hot path** (narration may reference only entities in the PerceptionFrame; effects satisfy the SQL invariant catalog; checked incrementally against a running consistency checkpoint, never a full-history rescan), plus an **occasional LLM pass** (non-load-bearing) that reasons about flagged anomalies, flags them back to the offending agent before narration reaches the player, and writes the human report. Remains strictly read-only.

**Two-tier authoring (the safety model).** A runtime-authored mechanic is *per-chronicle sandbox data*, gated by the automated physics checks (so it can never create matter or become unreachable) — "good enough to play, blast radius of one chronicle." Promotion to **canon** (shipped to all players via a Flyway migration/patch) is reserved for **human review**. Defense-in-depth: deterministic gate (physics floor) → QA critic (plausibility filter) → human (certifies canon). No LLM is ever the final authority.

**Discovery review pipeline.** Each discovery lands in a `chronicle_tech_discovery` ledger (source of truth) with the drafted rows, the gate result, and the QA verdict. The **Overseer** (`?mode=overseer`) gains a Tech Discovery Queue panel for live human review with Approve→migration / Reject. On promotion, a **GitHub issue** is auto-filed (labelled `tech-discovery`) with the migration draft + QA report — the durable, patch-linked record, and the surface a Claude Code collaborator can read to help finalise the patch. Raw discoveries are not auto-filed to GitHub (noise); only promotion candidates are.

**Cost shape.** AI fires once per novel procedure per chronicle before canonisation, and drops to **zero** for that mechanic after the patch — cost scales with the world's finite, converging vocabulary, not with play. Common actions (the ~99%) never touch an AI at all.

**Latency / UX.** A proportional loading state: near-instant (no overlay) for deterministic/common actions; a full unclickable centered loading overlay only while a genuine discovery pipeline runs. The multi-agent department is *not* faster per-action than one model — the speed advantage is that the common case is deterministic; the department convenes only for real invention.

### Reasoning

The prior "authoring-time only" rule was justified by measured arithmetic at ~5–10% mechanic coverage: most misses were *missing mechanics*, so a resolution-time model would pay to confirm emptiness. At 129 processes that premise has flipped — the belt and the planks already exist, and observed playthrough failures are increasingly *interpretation/decomposition* failures against present mechanics, plus deterministic bugs. Hand-authored recipes cannot cover the combinatorial space of valid real-world procedures, and the core philosophy (the player brings their real self; a chronicle survives by what they genuinely know) *demands* open-ended interpretation. The three arguments that still hold — nearest-neighbour false-matches, a stochastic step in front of a permanent record, and unreviewed canon — are each neutralised structurally: the deterministic gate rejects false-matches; runtime authoring writes only scoped, sandboxed, physics-gated data (never canon, never schema, never an outcome); and canon promotion stays human-gated. The QA critic and the split integrity gate raise quality without any LLM becoming load-bearing.

### Impact

- **Schema:** a nullable `discovered_by_chronicle_id` scope column on mechanic-definition tables (`material_process`, `item_definition`, …), added once, globally (consistent with DR-0013); resolution reads `WHERE discovered_by IS NULL OR discovered_by = <this chronicle>`. New `chronicle_tech_discovery` ledger. No runtime schema changes — the Architect inserts rows only.
- **Runtime:** the deterministic V53-style mass-balance/reachability gate becomes callable at insertion time (not only at migration/review). The author↔critic loop is capped at 2 rounds. The Auditor's hot-path integrity check is deterministic and incremental.
- **Boundaries reaffirmed/added:** Narrator never hints (DR-0010). Interpreter composes existing only. Architect authors scoped data, never schema/canon/outcomes. QA judges plausibility, never physics, and is a model independent of the Architect. Auditor stays read-only. New domains (new tables) remain human migrations (DR-0009/DR-0013).
- **Surfaces:** Overseer Tech Discovery Queue panel; `chronicle_tech_discovery` + a read endpoint; GitHub `tech-discovery` issues auto-filed on promotion only. Frontend proportional loading overlay.
- **Amends:** routing-and-coverage-strategy.md's "never at resolution time" for the composition/authoring case, with the arithmetic-flipped rationale recorded here. Extends DR-0009 (three-layer boundaries) with the Interpreter and QA roles under the non-load-bearing invariant.
- **Deferred to implementation spec** (architecture/runtime-procedure-authoring.md): per-role model assignment (Narrator=Haiku, Architect=Opus, QA=an independent strong model, Interpreter/Auditor-summary tiered by cost), prompt contracts, the discovery→promotion state machine, and the dedup of pending drafts across chronicles.
- **Does not change** the deterministic-first cost model (DR-0015/0016/0017): the ~99% common case still resolves with no AI call.

## DR-0022

**Date**

2026-08-04

**Category**

Simulation / AI Reasoning / Data Model / Reachability

**Status**

Accepted — implementation staged (tracker: [06.5-Phase0-Parity-Build-Plan.md](06.5-Phase0-Parity-Build-Plan.md))

### Decision

**Exceed and fix** the **Phase 0** (single-LLM ChatGPT) playthrough, not merely match it — this is the stated purpose of building and funding the Phase 1 engine (user, 2026-08-04). Keep everything that made Phase 0 rich (say-yes to rational actions, thick evolving objects, kingdom-building) **while fixing what Phase 0 structurally could not do**: true persistence, referential integrity, no self-contradiction, no hallucinated matter, exact numerical accounting, reproducibility. A review of the Phase 0 world-state database (13 JSON files from a real Day 1–10 "Wolf Kingdom" playthrough) plus the Day-6–10 playtest issues (#29–#37) establish four locked principles. All four sit *under* DR-0021's supreme invariant (every AI role optional and non-load-bearing over a complete deterministic core) and leave DR-0009/DR-0013 intact (no runtime schema changes; the Architect writes scoped data rows only).

1. **Numerical quantities are canonical, always.** Every item and material carries an exact integer count. Phase 0's qualitative pools ("Abundant / Moderate / Available") are **explicitly rejected** — they are not the goal. The richness Phase 0 had comes from AI *reasoning*, not loose accounting. Phase 1's precise inventory stays and is a strength.

2. **Reachability — not carried load — defines what an action may use.** What a chronicle can use is *everything physically within reach from where it stands*: carried items, items nested in carried containers, loose materials on the ground at its location, **and the contents of any storage/container sited at its current location/zone** — for **materials and tools alike**, each surfaced with its **count and source**. There must be **one** reachability model applied consistently to input-sourcing, tool checks, and the AI's context (today three code paths disagree, and on-site storage contents are unreachable — the known "documents on a stone shelf can't be read" limitation is the same root cause). Real-world basis: you pick stones and branches from around you, pull material from a bin you are next to, and take a tool off the rack by the bench. Scales to the tool-shed/rack phase via sub-location **zones** (a tool in a rack is reachable because you are at that workstation zone).

3. **Templates are guides for the AIs, not walls.** The 130 deterministic processes are **exemplars** the Interpreter and Architect pattern new authoring on — not the closed set of what is possible. A player doing something rational and realistic (with materials on hand or in reach, a stated procedure, even stated dimensions/features) must be *engaged by the pipeline*, never dismissed with the generic one-line miss.

4. **Author-with-precision → audit → persist.** On a rational request: the **Interpreter** first composes existing processes; if none fit, the **Architect** authors a NEW scoped mechanic with **exact numerical inputs** (drawn from the reachable pool), physics-gated (mass balance/reachability) → **QA** (plausibility) → recorded in `chronicle_tech_discovery` → **Auditor** validates the written rows → the object persists with **accurate numerical data**, grab-able later. Bespoke, *evolving* objects (a hand-carry basket **with a handle**, its dimensions, capabilities, and a dated modification log — the Phase-0 "Utility Belt Revision III") are represented by an **additive object-attributes layer** on `world_object` that never touches the mass-balance core.

### Reasoning

Phase 0 felt richer for two reasons: it said *yes* to any rational action, and every object was a thick, evolving entity with capabilities/provenance/dimensions/a modification history. But the Phase 0 prompt itself admits it "lacks persistence memory and data integrity" — it would craft from nothing, contradict itself, and lose state. Phase 1 bought real persistence, real physics, and no hallucinated matter — at the cost of the emergent richness. The fix is **not** to loosen accounting; it is to make the AI reasoning layer (already designed in DR-0021) actually *fire* and reason over a **physically-true, quantified picture of what is reachable**, author rational requests with exact bookkeeping, and let objects be thick. Verified today: the authoring machinery already writes exact `material_process_input` quantities and records discoveries — but it is double-gated off, the Architect receives `DISTINCT item_key` (counts-blind) and cannot reach on-site storage, the templates are not fed as exemplars, and a rich multi-step action collapses to the ambiguous one-line miss.

### Impact

- **Reachability:** one unified CTE that includes the contents of containers/storage sited at the chronicle's location/zone (materials **and** tools), each with count + source; routed through `hasAtLeast`/`consumeOne`/`reachableItemKeys` and the tool checks. Fixes the stone-shelf limitation. Deterministic — no key needed.
- **AI context:** Interpreter/Architect receive a **quantified, sourced** reachable pool (not bare keys) and the 130 processes as **exemplars**.
- **Data model:** an **additive** `object_attribute` / `object_modification` / capability layer on `world_object` — additive metadata, does not alter mass balance or the item catalogue.
- **Miss path:** stops returning the generic one-liner when the pipeline is available; engages the Interpreter/Architect.
- **Kingdom-building payoff (found on review):** workstations must **enable/improve** their crafts (today they have no mechanical effect — F7), and a settlement must support **many named zones per location** (today `DESIGNATE` is one-name-per-chunk — F8), with a planned→operational lifecycle. This is the Phase-0 mid-game loop and is required to *exceed* it. Tracked as Layer 4b in 06.5.
- **Verification gate:** "ready" is reported only when the acceptance suite (06.5, A1–A10, drawn from the real Phase-0 vocabulary) passes on a booted stack — deterministically where possible, through the tuned pipeline for the authoring cases. Plan completion is not readiness.
- **Operator gate:** the master + authoring switches (`DRAUGR_AI_ENABLED`, `authoring-enabled`) remain the operator's to flip with the key; the deterministic pieces (reachability, counts, exemplars, attributes, better miss) land key-free first so the loop is ready the moment the key is set.
- **Canon-promotion ownership = the Auditor, not the Architect.** The Architect only ever writes *scoped* data (never canon/schema — DR-0009/0013), so the read-only integrity role drives promotion: once a scoped discovery has run consistently, the **Auditor** validates its persisted rows and surfaces it as a canon-promotion candidate (integrity report + migration draft) into the Tech Discovery Queue; a **human approves** and the V53 gate + Flyway finalize. This applies uniformly to **both** producers — the Architect's authored *mechanics* and the Interpreter's learned *aliases/composites* (F9) are promoted the same way, by the Auditor. The Auditor prepares/drives promotion but is never the final authority.
- **Clarifies (does not overturn) DR-0021** — these are the execution principles beneath it, plus the promotion-ownership assignment above. Preserves DR-0009/DR-0013 (no runtime schema changes), the human-gated canon rule, and the deterministic-first cost model (DR-0015/0016/0017).

### Principles settled during Layer 4b (2026-08-04) — do not re-litigate

Two design principles were reasoned out with the user from real-world simulation truth, and are now locked. Both exist to *prevent restriction*, the thread's whole purpose.

**A. Reachability is knowledge-scoped, and hard-capped at the locality.** An action is a *goal carried out over time*, not a snapshot of arm's reach. A chronicle who knows their own settlement (built the zones, lived there, or carries a map) fetches what a task needs as part of the act — "craft a chest box" silently walks to the wood store and the tool shed and back. So the gate on what an action may use is **knowledge/memory (+ a carried map), not distance**; the cost of the walking is **time**, folded into the action's duration, never a failure. **The hard cap:** one action folds in fetching only within the **current chunk = the local settlement**. Materials in another chunk/region are **never auto-fetched** — the engine must not silently spend years crossing the map, however computable; beyond the locality is a **journey the player chooses** (a real travel + haul). Consequence: **Layer 1's chunk-scoped reachability is correct and must NOT be tightened to the current zone** — it *is* the locality cap, now justified by knowledge rather than arm's reach. Multi-zone (F8) adds naming/organization/navigation/fetch-time *on top* of unchanged chunk-wide reach; it is not a reachability restriction. A good *rejection* narration ("what is missing is not here — it lies wherever you last set it down"; later, naming the specific store) is what makes this legible — a "no" is a decision, not a wall.

**B. Workstations are efficiency, never a gate; quality is majorly the craftsman.** Ancient people made superior work — fine joinery, dressed stone, fine cloth — with basic tools, their hands, the ground, cord, and pegs. So **quality (grade) stays majorly the craftsman**: skill (mastery) + care + materials; a skilled, careful chronicle makes *fine* work **bare-handed**, and mastery raises that ceiling. A workstation therefore **never gates a craft and never carries its grade.** Its payoff is **efficiency** (less waste, and — as follow-up wiring — less time/effort), plus a **minor, bounded quality assist** (a stable held surface aids precision at the margin: the attempt is lifted one step, still capped against the materials by `worst()`, so it never rescues poor work or exceeds what hands and stock allow). Shipped as V69. Gating crafts behind infrastructure would re-introduce exactly the restriction (#37) this thread removes.

## DR-0023

**Date**

2026-08-20

**Category**

Gameplay / Maintenance & Decay (EPIC #215, story #220)

**Status**

Accepted — use-based wear shipped (V139, PRs #299/#300); time-based degradation DESIGNED and DEFERRED (spec below)

### Decision

Tools and items carry a real **maintenance need**, not a cosmetic label. This has two halves; the first is shipped, the second is designed here and deferred to a focused slice.

**Half 1 — use-based wear (SHIPPED).** `item_instance.condition_state` (`SOUND → WORN → BROKEN → DESTROYED`) was mendable (whetstone/repair) and examined, but nothing wore a tool through use. Now a tool accrues use and its edge/head steps down at thresholds. Model, deliberately uniform across paths:

- **Counter:** `item_instance.use_count` (V139), a general per-instance wear counter (so any tool wears on any use path).
- **Stepping:** on a successful tool-use, `use_count++`; at **8** the tool steps `SOUND→WORN`, at **16** `WORN→BROKEN`, kept in history as a `TOOL_WORN` `object_transition`. A **WORN** tool still works; a **BROKEN** tool is **refused** ("mend it first").
- **Repair resets wear:** honing/binding sets `use_count=0` (and `BROKEN→WORN`, else `→SOUND`), closing the wear↔repair loop — the counter is *uses since last mend*, not lifetime.
- **Drivers wired:** felling (`fellTree`, #299) and the shared crafting core (`executeProcess`, all CUTTING/STRIKING/AXE material processes, via `soundestToolOfClass`, #300). The gate now picks the **soundest** tool in reach and rejects a broken one, where before any tool of any condition passed.
- **Ripple rule (learned):** a fixed low threshold is safe for single-use tests but **not** for high-iteration *statistical* tests that run one tool through 100+ operations (`WoodenMalletRivingIntegrationTest` rives 120 logs on one axe). Such a test measures a different axis (yield, not durability) and must keep its tool keen across the run; wear is proven in dedicated tests instead. Grep loop *helpers* (`runs=`, extracted rive/craft loops), not only inline `for`.

**Half 2 — time-based degradation (DESIGNED, DEFERRED).** Exposed items should **rot/rust over time** the way built structures weather (#220 structure-weathering, V-earlier). This is deferred, not skipped, because two facts make it a designed slice rather than a safe tail-edit, and recording them here prevents re-derivation:

1. **No material signal exists.** `item_definition.category` is purpose (`MATERIAL/TOOL/FOOD/CONTAINER/CLOTHING/WEAPON`), not substance — it cannot separate organic (rots) from metal (rusts) from stone/fired (durable). Time-degradation therefore needs a **material-class mapping** first (a new column or a curated lookup), e.g. `ORGANIC` (wood/hide/fibre/bone), `METAL` (iron/copper), `MINERAL` (stone/fired clay = effectively durable). Food is already handled by the preservation subsystem and is **out of scope**.
2. **It is tick-wide and long-play-sensitive.** Degrading every *exposed* item (unowned, sitting at a `current_location_id`, not carried or in a container) each tick will interact with `LongPlayDisturbanceCycleIntegrationTest` (advances **30 sim-days**) and any test that leaves stock lying out. The rate must be **slow and material-scaled** (organic faster, metal slower, mineral ~never), tuned so ordinary test spans (hours–days) don't degrade, and only genuinely-abandoned goods rot over weeks. Carried/stored items are protected (the reason storage and a dry store matter). Degradation feeds the **same** `condition_state` ladder + `TOOL_WORN`/collapse history, and terminal rot ends in `DESTROYED` with a recorded cause (Auditor requires it).

### Reasoning

`condition_state` was a display-only token — the exact "inert catalogue" defect the real-world-simulation mandate targets: a state that reads against nothing. Wear makes it terminal (a maintenance need feeding the existing repair/hone loop) and gives tool-making, whetstones, and the dry store a reason to exist. Keeping wear **use-based and uniform** (one counter, one threshold rule, one repair reset) avoids per-tool special-casing and lets every future tool-use path opt in by calling one helper. Time-based rot is genuinely valuable (abandon a hide and it spoils; leave an iron blade in the wet and it rusts) but its clean version needs the material taxonomy and a ripple-safe rate — forcing it tick-wide without those, especially against a 30-day long-play test, is how subtle bugs enter the most-tested code. Deferring it to a designed slice is the disciplined call, and this record is its spec.

### Impact

- **Shipped:** V139 (`use_count`); `PhysicalItemService.fellTree` + `executeProcess` wear + broken-gate; `repairNamedItem` wear-reset; regressions `ToolWearIntegrationTest`, `ToolWearCraftingIntegrationTest`; `WoodenMalletRivingIntegrationTest` updated to hold its axe keen.
- **Next slice (unblocked by this spec):** a material-class mapping + a slow, material-scaled, exposure-only time-degradation in the world tick, with an explicit ripple pass against the 30-day long-play test. Extend use-wear to the remaining one-off tool crafts (`craftFireKit`/`craftPrimitiveSpear`/`craftFishingNet`, currently `hasCuttingTool`-gated but not wearing) at the same time or separately.
- Reaffirms DR-0022's "objects are thick, evolving entities with a modification history"; leaves DR-0009/DR-0013 intact (no runtime schema changes — this is a human migration).
