# 09.1 – Flora Catalogue

> **Project Draugr**
>
> *Everything that grows belongs somewhere.*

---

# Purpose

This document catalogues all flora species in Project Draugr.

Each entry defines the plant's ecological identity: what it is, where it grows, what it produces, and under what conditions it can be gathered or processed.

Flora is not decoration.

Every plant occupies an ecological role, influences surrounding life, and responds to season, climate, and disturbance.

---

# Schema Reference

Flora is implemented through three tables introduced in V39:

- `flora_definition` — the canonical species record
- `flora_drop` — what a species yields under what conditions
- `chunk_flora` — population of a species within a world chunk

A flora site uses `ecology_site.site_category = 'FLORA'`.

---

# Organism Types

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| TREE | Woody perennial, felled with axe-class tool |
| SHRUB | Woody but small, gathered bare-hand or knife |
| HERB | Non-woody ground plant, bare-hand harvest |
| MUSHROOM | Fungal organism, bare-hand, damp/shaded sites |
| AQUATIC | Water-rooted or water-margin plant |
| VINE | Climbing / trailing plant |
| GRASS | Ground-covering graminoid |

# Ecological Interactions

## Pollination

Honeybee colonies in the same chunk increase flora `regrowth_days` by 30%.

## Seed Dispersal

Field mouse and red squirrel populations in a chunk slowly spread hazel and oak to adjacent chunks over many seasons.

## Decomposition

Dead trees become mushroom sites after 30 in-game days without player intervention.

## Poison Identification

The narrator never explicitly identifies a plant as poisonous. Players who know their botany survive. Players who do not must learn through consequence or through reading another chronicle's written knowledge.
